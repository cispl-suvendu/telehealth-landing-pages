Frequently Asked
Clinical Questions

Our triage team is available 24/7 for technical support. For medical emergencies, please dial 911 or visit the nearest ER.

How do prescriptions work?

Doctors send electronic prescriptions (eRx) directly to your preferred local pharmacy via Surescripts integration.

Is my medical data secure?

We use AES-256 bit encryption and are fully HIPAA/HITECH compliant. Your data is never sold.

Can I get a lab referral?

Yes, our MDs can issue lab orders for Quest Diagnostics or LabCorp directly through the portal.

Do you treat emergencies?

No. We treat non-emergency acute conditions. If you have chest pain or severe trauma, call 911.
